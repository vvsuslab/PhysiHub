4

No English voice on the device

When: Audio uses the browser’s voice; some older devices have no English voice.

electromagnetic induction

/ɪˌlektrəʊmæɡˈnetɪk ɪnˈdʌkʃn/

no voice on this device — read the IPA instead

Rule: Hide the speaker button rather than leave one that makes no sound. A dead button looks like a fault.

5

Going offline

When: The site works offline, but it must say clearly what still works.

You are offline

Lessons, terms and practice tests still open. Audio needs a connection.

Rule: A slim banner under the header that disappears when you reconnect. No blocking pop-ups.

6

Loading

When: A slow device or connection; the glossary has not appeared yet.

Rule: Grey blocks in the shape of the coming content, not a spinner. Nothing jumps when the data arrives.

The common rule: say clearly what is happening, then always offer something the user can do next.
